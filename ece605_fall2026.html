ECE605 Large-Scale AI

Course Overview

The Fall 2026 offering focuses on the Embodied AI and Visual-Language-Action models. The course emphasizes real-world research problem-solving through three core pillars:

  • Literature Review: Role-playing presentations dissecting frontier across five thematic clusters:

  • Final Project Foundations & Interactive Simulation

  • Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Architectures

  • Robotics Data Systems & Scaling Laws

  • World Models & Latent Planning

  • Hybrid & Multimodal FrontiersVision-Language-Action (VLA) architectures and generative control policies.

  • Rigorous Experimentation: Hands-on empirical exploration, baseline reproduction, and model training on GPU clusters.

  • Collaborative Teamwork: Large-team engineering to build, iterate, and submit competitive solutions to a live benchmark.
